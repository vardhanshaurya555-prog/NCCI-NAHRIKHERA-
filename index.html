Welcome to NCCI

Decades old board of cricket

NCCI — Nahrikhera Cricket Council of India — celebrates cricket, competition, team spirit and the achievements of its players. This platform brings tournament announcements, player statistics, rankings, team standings and match updates together in one place.

Our aim is to give every team and player a place in the NCCI cricket story.
